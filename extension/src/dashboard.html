Adds Save to ShopMarx to the share sheet in Safari and in shop apps. A shared link becomes an item on the next sync.

  1. On the iPhone, open the shortcut and tap Add Shortcut.

The token lets the shortcut write to the ShopMarx folder in your Dropbox; keep it to yourself. Reset to default in this menu, or removing the app in Dropbox's settings, stops it working.

What ShopMarx can do

One place for everything you might buy, from any online shop: saved in a click, sorted your way, found in a second, with its price watched for you.

Save from any shop

  • One click on the toolbar icon, Alt+Shift+S, or right-click → Save this product.
  • Reads the product for you: name, pictures and price from the page's product data, with the largest price on the page as a fallback.
  • Point it out once: a shop shows the price or name somewhere odd? Click the right text on the page; ShopMarx remembers that spot for the shop.
  • Pick the picture from all the ones on the page, add a note, choose lists or type new ones.
  • Remembers your last lists for the next save; ⌘/Ctrl+Enter saves from anywhere in the popup.
  • Clean links: tracking parameters (utm, gclid, fbclid, Amazon and AliExpress leftovers…) are stripped, so the same product is never saved twice.

Organise your way

  • Lists and sub-lists at any depth; an item can be in several lists at once.
  • Drag and drop: cards onto lists, lists up, down and into other lists; or use Move to….
  • Select many cards, then move or delete them together.
  • Rename in place with a double-click or F2; sort sub-lists A–Z in one go.
  • Shops on their own: every shop your items come from gets a row, with lists of its own inside.
  • Tags on every card show the other lists an item is in; click one to jump there.
  • List, grid and compact views (⌘/Ctrl+1/2/3), sorting by date, price, price drop, shop or name.

Find anything fast

  • Just start typing anywhere: the letters go into the search.
  • Searches titles, shops, notes and list names; matching lists show as chips above the results.
  • Search commands: [list], -word, "exact phrase", shop:, price<50, price:20-50, has:note, no:price, is:cheaper.
  • Autocomplete finishes list names, shops and commands; a finished list turns into a tag, removed with one Backspace.

Prices that work for you

  • Price history for every item, with the drop or rise since you saved it on the card.
  • Update prices reads the current price of everything in view from the shops.
  • Any currency: prices are converted to the one you choose, at the European Central Bank's daily rates.

Good-looking pictures

  • Choose how shop photos show: grey tile, white tile, as the shop shows, or background removed (white furniture included).
  • Picture copies are kept on your devices and in Dropbox, so they still show offline or after a shop removes them.

Remember what you browsed (optional)

  • Visited pages: product pages you open in Chrome are saved on their own into Visited, kept apart from your collection.
  • Forgotten after 7, 30 or 90 days unless you keep them; pause any time; private windows are never recorded.

Made for the keyboard

  • Arrows move through lists, cards, list chips, every button at the top and inside every dialog and menu.
  • Tab goes round lists, cards and search; ⌘/Ctrl+K jumps to search.
  • Undo and redo everything: ⌘/Ctrl+Z and ⌘/Ctrl+Shift+Z.

Everywhere you are

  • Dropbox sync: the same items on every computer and phone; changes made in two places are merged item by item.
  • iPhone web app from the Home Screen, plus a share-sheet shortcut to save links from Safari and shop apps.
  • Backups: a backup folder kept up to date after every change, and Export / Import as one JSON file.
  • Light and dark mode, following your system or your choice.

Private by design

  • No account, no server, no tracking: your data lives in your browser and, if you want, in your own Dropbox app folder, where ShopMarx sees nothing else.
  • Access to shop pages is asked for only when a feature needs it.
  • A Chrome extension (Manifest V3) and a web app from the same code: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no frameworks, no build step.

How to use ShopMarx

Save a product

  • On a product page, click the ShopMarx icon.
  • Or press Alt+Shift+S, or right-click → Save this product to ShopMarx.
  • Check the title, picture and price, then Save.
  • Choose lists, or type a new one.
  • The bubble button adds a note.
  • Wrong price or name? Click the target button, then the right text on the page.
  • Saved before? Save again to update it.

Your items

  • Open this page from the logo in the popup.
  • Lists are on the left. + adds one; ⋯ renames or removes it. Double-click a list (or press F2) to rename it right there.
  • Search, sort and switch views at the top.
  • Point at a card to edit or delete it.
  • Tick cards to select several.
  • Drag cards onto a list, or onto Trash.
  • + in the top bar adds an item from a link.
  • Update prices in settings checks current prices.
  • Visited pages in settings (off at first): product pages you open in Chrome are saved on their own into Visited. Ones you leave only there are forgotten after the days you chose; move one to a list to keep it.
  • Keyboard: Tab goes round the lists, the cards and the search box. The arrows move inside each, and past the edge to the next: → from a list to the cards, ← from the first card in a row back to the lists, ↑ from the top row to the search, ↓ from the search to the results. The buttons and dropdowns at the top are on the way too: an arrow goes to the nearest one in that direction (on a dropdown, Space opens it).
  • Ctrl+Z undoes the last change; Ctrl+Shift+Z (or Ctrl+Y) redoes it.

Search

  • Just start typing anywhere on the page: the letters go into the search.
  • Words search titles, shops, notes and list names; every word must match. The search looks in the list that is open; add [all] to look everywhere.
  • "desk lamp": this exact phrase.
  • [home]: items in the list or shop home and its sub-lists, from everywhere. [all] is every item, [unsorted] those in no list.
  • -word, -"phrase", -[list]: leave those out, e.g. [all] -ali.
  • shop:ikea: from shops with ikea in their name.
  • price<50, price>20, price:20-50: by price, in the currency you show prices in.
  • has:note, has:price, has:picture, has:list, or no: for the opposite.
  • is:cheaper / is:pricier: the price went down / up since you saved it.
  • Mix them: [home] lamp price<30 -ikea.
  • While you type, suggestions finish lists, shops and commands: type [gi and press Enter or Tab for [Gift ideas]; for a plain word, press ↓ first. Esc hides them. A finished [list] becomes a tag in the box: Backspace at the start of the text removes the last tag, its × that one. Copying all of the box copies the whole search, tags included.

Keep a copy

  • Export / Import: one JSON file with everything.
  • Backup folder: a copy saved after every change.

Sync and phone

  • Dropbox sync: the same items on all your devices.
  • First time: follow the steps and paste your App key.
  • iPhone: open this page in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • The phone saves links; prices fill in on the computer.

Install

  • Download Chrome extension (.zip) and unzip it.
  • chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked → the extension folder.
