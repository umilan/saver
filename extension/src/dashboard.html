Connect Dropbox

Sync goes through your own Dropbox app. Once per Dropbox account:

  1. In the Dropbox App Console, create an app: Scoped access, App folder.
  2. Permissions: tick files.content.read and files.content.write, then Submit.
  3. Settings → OAuth 2: allow public clients, and add this redirect URI:
  4. Copy the App key from Settings and paste it here. It is kept on this device only.

iPhone shortcut

Adds Save to ShopMarks to the share sheet in Safari and in shop apps. A shared link becomes an item on the next sync.

  1. On the iPhone, open the shortcut and tap Add Shortcut.
  2. When it asks, paste the two values below. Opening this on the iPhone (the web app) makes copying them easiest.
  3. In any share sheet, tap Save to ShopMarks. The first time, allow it to connect to Dropbox.

The token lets the shortcut write to the ShopMarks folder in your Dropbox; keep it to yourself. Reset to default in this menu, or removing the app in Dropbox's settings, stops it working.

How to use ShopMarks

Save a product

  • On a product page, click the ShopMarks icon.
  • Or press Alt+Shift+S, or right-click → Save this product to ShopMarks.
  • Check the title, picture and price, then Save.
  • Choose lists, or type a new one.
  • The bubble button adds a note.
  • Wrong price or name? Click the target button, then the right text on the page.
  • Saved before? Save again to update it.

Your items

  • Open this page from the logo in the popup.
  • Lists are on the left. + adds one; ⋯ renames or removes it.
  • Search, sort and switch views at the top.
  • Point at a card to edit or delete it.
  • Tick cards to select several.
  • Drag cards onto a list, or onto Trash.
  • + in the top bar adds an item from a link.
  • Update prices in settings checks current prices.

Keep a copy

  • Export / Import: one JSON file with everything.
  • Backup folder: a copy saved after every change.

Sync and phone

  • Dropbox sync: the same items on all your devices.
  • First time: follow the steps and paste your App key.
  • iPhone: open this page in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • The phone saves links; prices fill in on the computer.

Install

  • Download Chrome extension (.zip) and unzip it.
  • chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked → the extension folder.
